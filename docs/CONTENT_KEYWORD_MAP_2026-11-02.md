## Editorial rules
- Use one primary intent per page and a small supporting cluster.
- Do not force every variant into visible copy.
- Prefer natural phrasing and project-specific nouns over generic SEO language.
- Do not repeat location terms unless they help user intent.
- Use services, headings, captions, metadata, alt text and internal links to distribute semantic context.
- Preserve chronology and story in narrative work.
- Never invent proof, metrics, testimonials, awards, clients or outcomes.
