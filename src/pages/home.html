THE WAY I WORK

How I work.

From the first conversation to the final handover.

Start with the right question.
  1. 01 / WORKING NOTES

    Discover

    I understand the real business problem, audience, and opportunity.

    In practice +

    Before opening a design file, I ask what needs to change. I look at your audience, competitors and business goals, then agree on a brief that gives the work a purpose.

  2. 02 / WORKING NOTES

    Define

    I translate insights into strategy and creative direction.

    In practice +

    I explore the strongest ideas and show the thinking behind each route. I help you choose a direction for what it communicates, then define the visual principles that will guide the work.

  3. 03 / WORKING NOTES

    Create

    I explore, design, prototype, and refine.

    In practice +

    This is where the idea has to hold up. I develop the identity or digital experience, refine it with your feedback and check the details in the situations where people will use it.

  4. 04 / WORKING NOTES

    Deliver

    I validate quality, prepare implementation, and support launch.

    In practice +

    The handover should make your next step easier. You receive organised files and guidance for using them, with launch or ongoing support agreed around what your team needs.

A few things worth knowing before we start.

Can I contact Hadi about leadership roles or consulting?+

Yes. I welcome conversations about creative and product design leadership roles, alongside consulting through INTELLIGNCE. Review my CV for experience in creative direction, team leadership, stakeholder communication and product delivery. Consulting can include brand strategy and guidelines, UI/UX and SaaS interfaces, landing pages, campaign direction or MVP planning. Explore the digital design collection, Morganics brand system or Social Media Portfolio to see the work.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes. I work remotely with founders and teams worldwide. We agree on milestones, review points and a communication rhythm that works across time zones.

How is a project priced?+

Each proposal is based on the scope, deliverables, research and production involved. After discussing your goals and budget, I outline the work, milestones and fees before the project begins.

Can you develop an existing brand?+

Yes. A rebrand can range from refining your logo and visual identity to building a broader brand system. We first identify what is working, what needs to change and where consistency is missing.

What should I send to get started?+

Send a short introduction to your business, the challenge you want to solve, your current brand or website, and your timeline and budget. Email hadi@intellignce.net or use WhatsApp to start the conversation.
