How we work.

Good work needs more than a good idea.
It needs a process that keeps everyone moving together.

Discovery

Before opening a design file, I ask what needs to change. We look at your audience, competitors and business goals, then agree on a brief that gives the work a purpose.

Ideation & Direction

I explore the strongest ideas and show the thinking behind each route. We choose a direction for what it communicates, then define the visual principles that will guide the work.

Design & Development

This is where the idea has to hold up. I develop the identity or digital experience, refine it with your feedback and check the details in the situations where people will use it.

Launch & Grow

The handover should make your next step easier. You receive organised files and guidance for using them, with launch or ongoing support agreed around what your team needs.

A few things worth knowing before we start.

Chat on WhatsApp ↗
What kinds of projects do you take on?+

Most projects begin with a launch, a rethink or a team that needs creative support. We can focus on one priority or connect several, such as an identity, a website and the campaign that introduces them.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes. I work remotely with founders and teams worldwide. We agree on milestones, review points and a communication rhythm that works across time zones.

How is a project priced?+

Each proposal is based on the scope, deliverables, research and production involved. After discussing your goals and budget, I outline the work, milestones and fees before the project begins.

Can you develop an existing brand?+

Yes. A rebrand can range from refining your logo and visual identity to building a broader brand system. We first identify what is working, what needs to change and where consistency is missing.

What should I send to get started?+

Send a short introduction to your business, the challenge you want to solve, your current brand or website, and your timeline and budget. Email hadi@intellignce.net or use WhatsApp to start the conversation.
