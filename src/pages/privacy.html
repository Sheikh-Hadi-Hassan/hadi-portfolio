Privacy & Portfolio Materials

How portfolio enquiries and assistant interactions are handled.

Project enquiries

Submitting an enquiry shares your reviewed brief, conversation and contact details with Hadi’s enquiry service. Drafts stay in this browser for up to seven days.

Optional AI and voice

Sera uses Google Gemini for project messages and recent context. Avoid confidential information. Voice recognition may use your browser provider’s speech service.

External services

Email, WhatsApp and social links open external services under their own privacy policies.

Portfolio materials and questions

Project artwork is displayed to present the work. Contact Hadi before reuse, or to ask about your enquiry data.
