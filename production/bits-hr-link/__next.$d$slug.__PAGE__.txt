<section class="case-introduction editorial-block"><h2>THE PROJECT</h2><p>Bits HR Link brings a technology-driven approach to human resources. The brand identity connects that ambition with a modern, energetic visual system, designed to communicate innovation, efficiency and professionalism across print, digital and physical environments.</p></section>
