THE PROJECT

Expertise needs a clear expression. This collection brings together LinkedIn carousel and post designs for professionals and businesses across healthcare, finance, construction, property and technology. Each visual direction gives its subject a recognisable presence while making information easier to follow.

The collection at a glanceFinance, with a clear point of viewPeople, expertise and trustPractical knowledge. A recognisable presenceTechnology in a visual languageA distinctive professional perspective
OVERVIEW

The collection at a glance.

A selection of visual directions for LinkedIn content, bringing strong opening ideas and consistent presentation into the feed.

01 / Finance

Finance, with a clear point of view.

Business readiness, payment psychology and finance topics use bold opening statements and structured layouts to create a clear visual sequence. Repeated colour and typography cues connect each series to its professional voice.

02 / Healthcare

People, expertise and trust.

Healthcare and workplace topics pair concise explanations with a consistent presentation. The layouts create room for professional perspective, educational content and people-focused communication.

03 / Construction Property

Practical knowledge. A recognisable presence.

Construction, property and business growth content brings practical topics into a repeatable editorial format. Contrasting headlines, numbered ideas and relevant imagery guide the reader from the opening statement to the supporting detail.

04 / Technology

Technology in a visual language.

Green-led compositions organise product and marketing ideas into a cohesive sequence, combining concise statements, diagrams and imagery.

05 / Professional Perspective

A distinctive professional perspective.

Career coaching, leadership, personal branding and wellbeing take distinct visual directions. Each series uses typography, space and supporting imagery to make its professional point of view recognisable.

PROJECT DELIVERABLES

  • LinkedIn carousel and social post designs
  • Sector-specific visual directions
  • Reusable typography, colour and layout approaches
  • 29 original presentation boards
