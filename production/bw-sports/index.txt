Product photography for BW Strike.</p><dl><dt>CLIENT</dt><dd>BW Strike</dd></dl><dl><dt>SERVICES</dt><dd>Product photography<br>Sports imagery<br>Product presentation</dd></dl><dl><dt>TYPE</dt><dd>Product photography</dd></dl></div></header>

<section class="case-introduction editorial-block"><h2>THE PROJECT</h2><p>BW Strike approached the project with two needs: present the detail of its boxing equipment and show how that equipment looks in use. The photography connects controlled product studies with a real boxing-gym setting, covering gloves, focus pads, training accessories and athlete portraits.</p></section>
<nav class="morganics-index" aria-label="Case-study chapters"><a href="#athletes-in-context">Equipment in its element</a><a href="#equipment-details">Closer to the craft</a><a href="#product-studies">Shape. Finish. Function</a><a href="#project-cover">The collection at a glance</a></nav>

<section class="editorial-block"><h2>The product shoot</h2><div><p>The original production notes describe a controlled studio setup using softbox lighting and a high-resolution DSLR with a macro lens. Multiple angles were used to show the construction, branding and visible details of boxing gloves and focus pads.</p></div></section><section class="editorial-block"><h2>Inside the gym</h2><div><p>The live-use photographs were made in a boxing gym, working with professional boxers. Faster shutter settings and continuous shooting supported the action sequences, while portable lighting supplemented the available light.</p></div></section><section class="editorial-block"><h2>Solving for movement and light</h2><div><p>The main production challenges were keeping illumination consistent in a changing environment and controlling motion blur during fast movement. The combination of supplemental lighting, camera settings and timing allowed the project to connect product detail with sporting context.</p></div></section>
