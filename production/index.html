How we work.

Good work needs more than a good idea.
It needs a process that keeps everyone moving together.

Discovery

Before opening a design file, I ask what needs to change. We look at your audience, competitors and business goals, then agree on a brief that gives the work a purpose.

Ideation & Direction

I explore the strongest ideas and show the thinking behind each route. We choose a direction for what it communicates, then define the visual principles that will guide the work.

Design & Development

This is where the idea has to hold up. I develop the identity or digital experience, refine it with your feedback and check the details in the situations where people will use it.

Launch & Grow

The handover should make your next step easier. You receive organised files and guidance for using them, with launch or ongoing support agreed around what your team needs.

Let’s Talk About Your Project
01.What kinds of projects do you take on?+

I work on projects where design needs to solve a real business problem. That includes brand strategy, visual identities, rebranding, packaging, creative campaigns, digital experiences, and video production. Whether you're launching something new or rethinking an established brand, I can help shape the idea and bring it to life.

02.Can we work together remotely?+

Absolutely. I'm based in Lahore, Pakistan, and work with businesses and teams internationally. I'm comfortable collaborating across time zones, managing projects remotely, and keeping communication clear from the first conversation to final delivery.

03.How do you approach a new project?+

I start by understanding your business, audience, competition, and objectives. Before designing anything, I want to know what we're trying to achieve and why it matters. From there, I develop a creative direction, build the visual system, and refine it into something that looks distinctive and works in the real world.

04.How is a project priced?+

Every project is different, so I don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing. My quotes depend on the scope, complexity, timeline, and level of creative involvement required. I offer project-based pricing, hourly engagements, and ongoing creative partnerships. You'll know what's included before we begin.

05.Can you improve an existing brand without starting over?+

Definitely. Not every brand needs a new logo. Sometimes the real problem is inconsistent communication, weak positioning, or a visual identity that hasn't evolved with the business. I'll evaluate what's working, identify what's holding the brand back, and recommend what actually needs to change.

06.Do you work independently or with a team?+

Both. I'm hands-on with strategy, design, and creative direction. For larger projects, I can also lead multidisciplinary teams through INTELLIGNCE, my creative and technology company. You get direct creative involvement from me, with additional expertise when the project requires it.

07.Can I hire you as a Creative Director or Design Lead?+

Yes. I'm open to freelance projects, creative leadership contracts, and suitable long-term opportunities. I bring over a decade of experience across brand development, campaigns, digital products, and team leadership. I can contribute strategically while staying involved in the actual creative work.

08.What should I send you to get started?+

Start with a conversation. Tell me what you're building, what challenge you're facing, and what you'd like to achieve. If you have a brief, references, timeline, or budget in mind, that's helpful too. You don't need to have everything figured out. That's part of what I can help you with.
